ClawApp Privacy Policy

This page is included inside ClawApp and is also intended to be published at https://clawapp.work/privacy.

English

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the ClawApp macOS application, the local support page, and support or privacy requests sent to support@clawapp.work or privacy@clawapp.work.

What ClawApp stores locally

  • OpenClaw configuration and runtime state inside the app sandbox container
  • User preferences such as language, appearance, quit behavior, and debug logging level
  • Onboarding transcripts stored in local debug logs
  • Optional runtime logs when the user enables Metadata Only or Full Content logging

What ClawApp sends over the network

  • Provider authentication and model traffic to services the user configures, such as OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic, or another compatible provider
  • Optional channel traffic if the user configures integrations such as Telegram
  • Support emails or privacy requests if the user contacts ClawApp by email

ClawApp does not include analytics or telemetry in v1. The local gateway binds to 127.0.0.1 by default.

Third parties that may receive data

  • The provider selected by the user, for example OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic, or another compatible model provider
  • Telegram, if the user configures Telegram integration
  • Email providers used to deliver support or privacy messages when the user contacts ClawApp by email

How long data is kept

  • Local app data stays on the user’s Mac until the user disconnects a provider, clears logs, resets the app, or removes the app and its container
  • Exported log archives are retained wherever the user saves them and are under the user’s control
  • Support or privacy emails may be retained for as long as needed to resolve the request, comply with legal obligations, or maintain support records

User choices, deletion, and revocation

  • Users can disconnect the active provider from Settings
  • Users can clear debug logs without resetting the entire app
  • Users can reset the app to remove sandboxed OpenClaw data and return to onboarding
  • Users can request deletion of support or privacy correspondence by emailing privacy@clawapp.work

Contact

For general support, email support@clawapp.work. For privacy requests, data deletion requests, or policy questions, email privacy@clawapp.work.

简体中文

适用范围

本政策适用于 ClawApp macOS 应用、本地支持页面，以及发送到 support@clawapp.work 或 privacy@clawapp.work 的支持与隐私请求。

ClawApp 在本地存储的内容

  • 保存在应用沙盒容器中的 OpenClaw 配置和运行状态
  • 语言、外观、退出行为和调试日志级别等用户偏好
  • 保存在本地调试日志中的引导过程记录
  • 当用户启用“仅元数据”或“完整内容”日志时产生的运行时日志

ClawApp 可能通过网络发送的内容

  • 发送到用户自行配置的提供商服务的认证和模型请求，例如 OpenAI、OpenRouter、Anthropic 或其他兼容提供商
  • 如果用户配置了 Telegram 等集成，相应的可选频道流量
  • 当用户通过电子邮件联系 ClawApp 时产生的支持或隐私请求内容

ClawApp v1 不包含分析或遥测功能。本地网关默认只监听 127.0.0.1。

可能接收数据的第三方

  • 由用户选择的模型提供商，例如 OpenAI、OpenRouter、Anthropic 或其他兼容提供商
  • 如果用户启用了 Telegram 集成，则包括 Telegram
  • 如果用户通过电子邮件联系支持或隐私地址，则包括用于投递邮件的电子邮件服务提供商

数据保留时间

  • 本地应用数据会一直保存在用户 Mac 上，直到用户断开提供商、清除日志、重置应用，或删除应用及其容器
  • 导出的日志压缩包保存在用户选择的位置，由用户自行控制保留时间
  • 支持或隐私邮件可能会在解决问题、履行法律义务或保留支持记录所需的时间内被保存

用户选择、删除与撤回

  • 用户可以在设置中断开当前提供商
  • 用户可以单独清除调试日志，而不重置整个应用
  • 用户可以重置应用，删除沙盒中的 OpenClaw 数据并返回引导流程
  • 用户可以发送邮件到 privacy@clawapp.work 请求删除支持或隐私沟通记录

联系方式

一般支持问题请发送邮件到 support@clawapp.work。隐私请求、数据删除请求或政策相关问题请发送到 privacy@clawapp.work。
